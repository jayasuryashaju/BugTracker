The product

Everything from “it’s broken” to “it’s fixed”, on one screen.

A focused tracker that covers the whole loop. Pick a view:

Know the state of play in five seconds. Open work, overdue and unassigned counts, created versus resolved over the last 14 days, and the status and priority mix — all live.

The workflow

Four steps. No ceremony.

  1. 1

    Capture

    Log it with markdown, repro steps and as many screenshots or recordings as it takes.

  2. 2

    Triage

    Set priority and a due date, tag it, link related bugs, and assign an owner.

  3. 3

    Resolve

    Discuss in threads, log time, and drag the card forward. Everyone involved is told.

  4. 4

    Ship

    Close it with a full audit trail, and watch the overdue count fall.

Questions

Good to know

How do my teammates join?

Invite them from the Team page with a role. When they sign up (or sign in with Microsoft) using the invited email address, they land in your workspace automatically. Colleagues on a company domain who weren’t invited are asked to request an invite.

What can I attach to a bug?

Images, screen recordings, PDFs and logs — up to 100 MB per file, as many as you like. Drag them in, browse, or paste a screenshot from your clipboard.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The mobile layout has a bottom tab bar, a swipeable board, bottom-sheet dialogs and touch-sized controls. It follows your phone’s light or dark setting.

Is my data separate from other organizations?

Yes. Every bug, project, tag and attachment belongs to one organization and is only visible to its members. Developers additionally see only the bugs assigned to or filed by them.

Can I sign in with Microsoft?

Yes — Microsoft sign-in is supported alongside email and password, including personal Microsoft accounts.

Can I export my bugs?

Any filtered list can be exported to CSV from the All bugs page.
