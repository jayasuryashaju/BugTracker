How It Works

From discovery
to resolution.

A streamlined workflow that keeps every bug moving forward — with full transparency and accountability at every step.

1
Tester or Admin logs the bug
Fill in the title, description, steps to reproduce, priority, project, and attach a screenshot or recording. Assign immediately or leave it for triage.
2
Manager assigns to a Developer
The Manager reviews the backlog, assigns the right developer, and sets priority. The developer receives an instant real-time notification.
3
Developer investigates and comments
Status moves to "In Progress". The developer logs work hours, uses threaded comments to share findings, and links related issues.
4
Bug is resolved and closed
Developer marks it Resolved. Tester verifies the fix and closes it. A complete activity log captures the full lifecycle permanently.
